what we offer

Language courses

We offer courses in German, Swiss German, and English, taught exclusively by native speakers. All levels are welcome, from complete beginners to advanced learners looking to refine their skills. If you are interested in a language not listed here, please get in touch — we may be able to help.

Exam preparation

We offer targeted preparation for internationally recognised language exams, including Cambridge, TOEFL, IELTS, TELC, and Goethe. Courses are tailored to the format and requirements of your chosen exam and are taught in the exam language. If you prefer to be taught in another language, please let us know and we will do our best to accommodate you.

Tutoring

We provide tutoring support across all educational levels, from primary school through to university. This includes Gymivorbereitung. Whether you need ongoing help or targeted preparation for an exam, we will work with you to find the right approach.

Our teachers

All of our teachers hold or are working towards a university degree and a formal teaching qualification (Lehrdiplom). Our language teachers specialise in linguistics, bringing an understanding of how language works - not just how to speak it - to every lesson.

Location

Classes are held in central Zürich, within easy reach of Zürich HB. The exact venue is confirmed when a course is scheduled. Private, company, and online sessions can of course take place wherever suits you best.

Course structure

As a general guide, progressing through a full level, for example from A1 to A2, typically takes around 50 hours* alongside a similar amount of independent study. Note that at higher levels, regular exposure and contact with native speakers becomes increasingly important alongside formal instruction.

Courses are typically offered in blocks of 30 or 60 sessions. The right block size depends on your goals and schedule — we will discuss this as part of your enquiry.

* A class hour at learning with gioia is 50 minutes. We therefore recommend approximately 60 sessions to complete one full level.

Pricing

Pricing depends on various factors, including the type of course, frequency, group size, and location. We will include a quote in our response to your enquiry.
