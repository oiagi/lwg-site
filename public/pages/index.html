what we offer

Language courses

Our language courses take place in a personal setting in our classrooms, at your offices, at your home or online. They are taught exclusively by native-speaking teachers.

Languages

  • German
  • Swiss German
  • English
  • Other languages For languages not listed here, send us an enquiry. We will find a suitable teacher within our network.
  • Preparing for a language certificate Goethe, TELC, FIDE, Cambridge, TOEFL, IELTS and more.

Format

  • Group Our group classes have between three and five participants, so that everybody gets to actively apply the target language during lessons.
  • One-to-one More flexibility and more opportunity to apply the target language.
  • Company courses Our company courses take place at your offices. Ideal for anyone short of time who has to work around a busy schedule.
  • Build your own Get in touch and we will find the right option for you.

Location

  • In person Our courses take place in Zürich. Message us to find out about our locations.
  • Online All of our courses are also offered fully online, or mixed with in-person lessons.

Prices for standard language courses

group (3-5) CHF 50 per person per 60 min
private CHF 120 per 60 min

A standard language course runs 32 × 60 min.

Company courses and tailored programmes are priced on request.

tutoring

Tutoring

We offer tutoring in every subject taught in Swiss schools.

Levels

  • Primary school
  • Secondary school
  • Gymnasium
  • BMS

Format

  • Group Taught in groups of three to seven pupils. Where the levels differ widely, a smaller group works better, so that every pupil gets the attention they need. Where levels and tasks are similar, a larger group works well.
  • One-to-one One-to-one lessons offer the most flexibility and allow for an individual pace of learning.

Location

  • In person Our courses take place in Zürich. Message us to find out about our locations.
  • Online All of our courses are also offered fully online, or mixed with in-person lessons.

Prices

group (3-7) CHF 80 per person per 60 min
private CHF 120 per 60 min
